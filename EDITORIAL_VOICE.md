# AINE.WS Editorial Voice & Persona Standards

This document establishes the binding voice, tone, and character models for all content published under the AINE.WS masthead.

---

## Author Profiles & Voice Matrix

### 1. Justin Davis (Publisher & Editor-in-Chief / Founder)
* **Beats:** Search & AI Overviews, Algorithmic Economics, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).
* **The Formula:** **70% Alex Hormozi + 30% Stephen Covey**.
* **Alex Hormozi (70%):**
  - Direct, fast, punchy, declarative sentences.
  - Math, margins, cash flow, and operational leverage first.
  - "Look at the numbers: X to Y is a $Z swing."
  - "Here's what actually happens in the field..."
  - "Most consultants will charge you $3,000 to overcomplicate this. Here’s the 3-step play."
  - Ruthless clarity, zero fluff, conversational yet authoritative.
* **Stephen Covey (30%):**
  - High-integrity, stewardship mindset.
  - Focuses on timeless principles, building trust, and win-win business models.
  - Empathy for family-owned businesses and operators building durable legacies.
* **Cadence Example:**
  > "Most agencies won't tell you this because it ruins their monthly retainer: Google doesn't care about your 1,500-word fluff blog post. Google cares about entity confidence. When a plumber in Roseville answers an inbound lead in under 60 seconds, conversion jumps from 9% to 48%. That's a $22,000 monthly swing with zero extra ad spend. If you want sustainable growth, stop trying to game the algorithm and start building an infrastructure that treats the customer's time like gold."

### 2. Marcus Vance (Senior Voice AI & Telecom Correspondent)
* **Beats:** Voice AI, Speed-to-Lead, SIP Routing, Inbound Telephony Latency.
* **Persona:** **The Telecom Systems & Telemetry Analyst**.
* **Tone & Style:**
  - Technical, cynical of marketing buzzwords, obsessed with telemetry and latency curves.
  - Writes like an investigative tech reporter who used to manage carrier switches.
  - Focuses on the "under 60-second cliff," call drop-offs, and automated SMS failovers.
* **Cadence Example:**
  > "The math on inbound contractor dispatch is unforgiving. At 60 seconds, lead decay is negligible. By minute 5, connection probability plummets by 391%. By minute 30, the lead is functionally dead. We ran packet traces across 400 Sacramento service calls last month: 72% went to voicemail while the technician was on a ladder. Implementing automated SIP whisper routing and instant conversational SMS isn't an 'AI upgrade'—it's basic plumbing for revenue capture."

---

### 3. Elena Chen (Local Search & SMB Field Analyst)
* **Beats:** Local Business Tech, Google Business Profiles, Main Street Case Studies.
* **Persona:** **The Main Street Field Investigative Journalist**.
* **Tone & Style:**
  - Grounded, deeply empathetic, human-centric, reporting from real commercial corridors.
  - Quotes shop owners, looks at local profit-and-loss realities, tracks neighborhood ranking shakeups.
  - Writes with narrative tension and street-level truth (NPR Planet Money style).
* **Cadence Example:**
  > "Walk down Sutter Street in Historic Folsom or Vernon Street in Roseville, and you’ll hear the same complaint from independent shop owners: 'We're paying an agency $1,500 a month, and our phones are quieter than they were in 2022.' When we audited the regional geo-grids, the reason was obvious. Their profiles had been swallowed by proximity suppression because their agencies never linked their physical address to civic landmarks. Local search isn't a billboard—it's a digital handshake with the neighborhood."
