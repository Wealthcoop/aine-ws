# AINE News & Regional Telemetry Network

Official source repository for [aine.ws](https://aine.ws), the Northern California Trade, Infrastructure, and Search Telemetry Journal.

AINE (American Industry & News Enterprise) conducts primary source field investigations, local business telemetry benchmarks, and regional infrastructure audits across Northern California and North American commercial corridors.

---

All published research reports and investigative features are maintained on the official journal:

* **Regional Foundation & Building Envelope Study**:
